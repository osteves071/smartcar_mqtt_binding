# Privacy Policy Last updated: August 2025 This application ("vw-id4-openhab") is a personal home automation project used by the owner to access vehicle information through the Smartcar platform. Data accessed may include: * Battery state of charge * Charging status * Vehicle location * Odometer readings * Other vehicle information explicitly authorized through Smartcar This information is used solely for display and automation within the owner's openHAB installation. No vehicle data is sold, shared, or provided to third parties. Vehicle information is stored only as necessary to operate the application and may be retained within the openHAB environment and related MQTT services. Users may revoke access at any time through Smartcar or the vehicle manufacturer's connected services. For questions regarding this application, contact the application owner.
